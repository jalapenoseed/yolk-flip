Yolk Flip

Your phone is the pan. Cook the ticket without breaking the yolk.

  • Sunny — no flip, set the white
  • Over Easy — one gentle flip, runny yolk
  • Over Medium — flip, jammy center
  • Over Hard — flip, yolk set, still intact

iPhone: tap Enable Gyro, then Allow. Desktop: drag the egg / swipe up to flip.
